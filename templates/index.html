Medical & Technical Concepts

About the Technology

Understanding pneumonia, radiographic diagnosis, and the power of attention mechanisms.

🫁

What is Pneumonia?

Pneumonia is an acute respiratory infection that inflames the air sacs (alveoli) in one or both lungs. The alveoli can fill with fluid, purulent matter, and cellular debris, causing cough, fever, chills, and impaired gas exchange.

🔬

What is a Chest X-Ray?

A chest radiograph is a non-invasive imaging study using low-dose electromagnetic radiation. Dense structures (bones) appear white, air-filled healthy lungs appear radiolucent (dark), and fluid-filled infected consolidations appear as hazy white or gray opacities.

🧠

What is a CNN?

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are specialized deep learning models equipped with spatial convolution kernels. They automatically learn hierarchical visual representations—ranging from edges and textures to complex anatomical structures.

🎯

What is Attention Mechanism?

Attention mechanisms dynamically assign non-uniform weights to feature maps, mimicking human visual cognition. Rather than treating all image pixels equally, the network selectively emphasizes informative lung regions while suppressing extraneous noise.

⚡

Why Attention Improves Image Analysis

In chest radiography, significant portions of the image contain irrelevant artifacts: clavicles, rib shadows, diaphragm, cardiac silhouette, and black background. Attention mechanisms help the network focus specifically on pulmonary parenchyma and subtle alveolar infiltrates, reducing false positives and enhancing interpretability via Grad-CAM heatmaps.
